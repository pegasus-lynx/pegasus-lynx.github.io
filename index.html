01 · Background

About Me

I’m Dipesh Kumar, a Software Engineer II at Microsoft in Hyderabad with 5+ years on the core team of Windows Presentation Foundation (WPF) and WinUI 3. Maintaining foundational .NET and Windows UI frameworks in the open (@dipeshmsft) means balancing decades-long compatibility with modern API design, community PR reviews, and open-source leadership.

Across my work at Microsoft and research at IIT (BHU) Varanasi, I enjoy moving across layers — from reactive UI controls, ETL/Kusto performance pipelines, and AI-assisted servicing agents to VS Code developer extensions, C++ OpenGL rendering, and published NLP research on subword tokenization.

Outside of engineering, you’ll usually find me reading fiction or essays over a cup of chai, experimenting with typography and visual design, or out on road trips and mountain trails with a camera — moments I collect in the Gallery.
